# Heat illness

Dizzy, weak, nauseous or with a headache, sweating or with hot skin, after hours in the sun or dancing.

1. If they are confused, speak strangely or faint, call 112 right away or ask someone to call.
2. Take them to shade or a cool place and help them lie down.
3. Loosen tight clothes and take off anything extra.
4. Cool them down: water on the skin and air, cold packs on the neck, in the armpits and in the groin.
5. If they are awake and able to swallow, give water or a drink with salts, in small sips.
6. If they do not feel better after 30 minutes, call 112. Keep cooling them.
7. If they stop responding, follow the sheet "Unresponsive but breathing normally" or "Unresponsive and not breathing normally".

! Do not give them anything to drink if they are vomiting or not awake. Do not give them alcohol.
