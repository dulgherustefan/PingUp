# Unresponsive but breathing normally

They do not react when you shout and tap their shoulders, but they are breathing normally.

1. Kneel next to them. Bend the arm nearest to you at a right angle and put the back of their other hand under their cheek.
2. Bend the far knee and pull on it towards you, until they roll onto their side.
3. Tilt the head slightly back, with the mouth facing the ground, so they can breathe freely.
4. Call 112 or ask someone to call.
5. Stay with them and watch their breathing until the team arrives.
6. If they stop breathing normally, turn them onto their back and follow the sheet "Unresponsive and not breathing normally".

! Do not leave them alone and do not leave them lying on their back. Do not give them anything to drink or eat.
