# Unresponsive and not breathing normally

They do not react when you shout and tap their shoulders. They are not breathing at all, or only gasping slowly.

! Slow gasping is not normal breathing. Start the compressions.

1. Shout for help. Ask someone to call 112 and keep the phone on speaker next to you.
2. Send someone for the nearest AED (defibrillator). On the map it is under "AED".
3. Lay them on their back, on a hard surface.
4. Put the heel of one hand on the middle of the chest and the other hand on top of it. Keep your arms straight.
5. Press the chest down 5–6 cm, 100–120 times a minute. Let the chest come back up after each press.
6. If you have been trained, give 2 rescue breaths after every 30 compressions. If not, just keep pressing, without pauses.
7. When the AED arrives, switch it on and do what it says. Do not touch the person while the device tells you not to.
8. Keep going until the medical team takes over or they start breathing normally. If someone is with you, swap every 2 minutes.
