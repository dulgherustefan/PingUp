# Heavy bleeding

Blood is flowing heavily or spurting from a wound.

1. Call 112 or ask someone to call.
2. If you have gloves, put them on. Otherwise, keep a clean bag or a piece of cloth between your hand and the blood.
3. Press hard, directly on the wound, with a clean material: a dressing, a T-shirt, a scarf.
4. Do not lift the material to look. If it soaks through, put another on top and keep pressing.
5. Keep pressing without a break until the medical team arrives.
6. Help them sit or lie down, keep them warm and talk to them.

! If an object is stuck in the wound, do not pull it out. Press around it.
