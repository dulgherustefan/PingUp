# The beach

What the flags mean, where the lifeguards are and how to avoid the dangers in the water.

## The flags
- Green: good conditions for swimming.
- Yellow: rough water or currents. Go in with care, stay close to the shore and do not swim far out.
- Red: swimming is forbidden. Do not go into the water.
- No flag, or another colour: ask a lifeguard before you go in.

On other beaches the flags can follow other rules. What counts are the ones of the lifeguards at the festival beach (source: organiser).

## The basic rules
- Do not go into the water at night. You cannot see the currents, and help takes long to arrive.
- Do not swim after alcohol or after taking other substances.
- Swim only in the zones with lifeguards and only during their hours.
- Do not swim alone.
- If a current pulls you out to sea, do not fight it straight back to shore. Swim parallel to the beach until you are out of the current, then towards the shore. Shout and wave an arm.

! If you see someone in trouble in the water, do not go in after them. Shout for a lifeguard, call 112 and throw them something that floats.
