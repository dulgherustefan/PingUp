## Looking after yourself
- Drink water regularly, especially in the sun and while dancing. Alcohol dries the body out.
- Eat something and take breaks in the shade.
- Alcohol, drugs and medicines can combine dangerously. If you have taken something, do not mix more.
- Substances from the illegal market can contain something other than what you were told. You never know the real dose.
- If you choose to use, start with a little, wait to see the effect and do not use alone.
- Tell someone you trust what you have taken.
- Do not drive after alcohol or other substances.

## When someone needs help at once
- They cannot be woken or do not respond.
- They breathe with difficulty, slowly or noisily, or their lips are blue.
- They have convulsions.
- Their skin is hot and dry, or they are confused or very agitated.
- They have chest pain.

The first aid sheets under Me say what to do until the medical team arrives.

! Do not leave a person in this state alone. Call 112 or call the medical team and say honestly what they took: it is needed to treat them.
