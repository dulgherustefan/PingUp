# Transport and the last ride

How to get to the festival, how to leave and how much time you have before the last ride.

## The last ride
- The time of the last ride to the station and to town: (source: organiser).
- The shuttle stop and route: (source: organiser).
- Extra trains or timetable changes: (source: organiser). For the regular timetable, check the official CFR Calatori timetable.

## Before you leave
- Find out the time of the last ride before the concerts and set an alarm 30 minutes ahead.
- Charge your phone in good time. The charging points are on the map.
- Agree with your friends where to meet and how you get home.

## Taxis
- Use licensed taxis, with the fare displayed on the door and the licence number in view.
- Ask the driver to start the meter, or agree the price before you get in.
- The official taxi rank at the festival: (source: organiser).

## If you missed the last ride
- Do not walk along roads without a pavement or without light.
- Go to the information point or to a member of staff. They will point you to other options (source: organiser).
- Stay with your friends or somewhere with people.

! If you do not feel safe, call 112. Discreet help in the app, when the organiser has switched it on, is under Report.
