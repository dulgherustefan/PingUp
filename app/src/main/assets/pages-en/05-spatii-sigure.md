# Safe spaces and Ask for Angela

Where to find help if you feel unsafe or harassed and want to leave a situation without explaining.

## Ask for Angela
- "Ask for Angela" is a discreet code that came from the United Kingdom: you ask for "Angela" at the bar, at info or at a steward, and the staff member understands you need help, without you explaining in front of others.

## In the app
- If the organiser has switched on Discreet help, you find it under Report, at "I feel unsafe". It sends your location silently to the safety team.
- The message travels through nearby phones with Ping Up and is not guaranteed. If you are in danger, go to a steward or call 112.

## If you are harassed or assaulted
- Go where there are people and light and ask a steward or the safety team for help.
- You have the right to say no and to leave. You do not have to explain.
- You can report later too, even if you do not want to now.

! Immediate danger: call 112.
