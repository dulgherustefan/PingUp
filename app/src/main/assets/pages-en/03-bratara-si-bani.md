# The wristband and money

How paying with the wristband works, where to top it up and what to do if you lose it.

## Paying with the wristband
- If the festival is cashless, you pay with the wristband (RFID), not with cash (source: organiser).
- The wristband is tied to your balance. Do not give it to anyone and do not leave it unattended.
- You check your balance here: (source: organiser).

- Keep the top-up confirmation until the festival is over.

## A lost or stolen wristband
- Go at once to the information point or to a top-up point.
- Ask for the wristband to be blocked, so that nobody spends your balance.
- Give the wristband number or the details you registered it with.
- Whether the balance moves to a new wristband: (source: organiser).

For a complaint about payments, write to the organiser first. After that you can turn to ANPC, the National Authority for Consumer Protection.

! If things were taken from you by threat or force, call 112.
